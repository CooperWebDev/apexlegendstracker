Apex Tracker

1. Get a free key at https://portal.apexlegendsapi.com (sign in, create a key).
2. Open js/config.js and paste the key between the quotes.
3. Open index.html in your browser.

Pages: index, players, compare, maps, ranked, news, status.
Note: the key is visible to anyone who views the site's source. For a public site, put the key behind a small server-side proxy.
